# SCITI Audit & Enhancement Plan

## Objective
Audit https://sciti.nonarkara.org for award submission readiness, test all features/languages, identify gaps, research smart city best practices, and produce a comprehensive critical report + content bible.

## Stage 1 — Full Website Audit (Orchestrator-led)
- Visit every page, click every link, test all language switches
- Test interactive features, forms, media, downloads
- Document UI/UX issues, bugs, broken elements
- Note content gaps and structural issues

## Stage 2 — Deep Backend & Technical Analysis
- Inspect source code, meta tags, SEO, accessibility
- Check performance indicators, mobile responsiveness
- Analyze architecture, security headers, load behavior
- Technical debt assessment

## Stage 3 — Smart City Content Research (deep-research-swarm)
- Research global smart city benchmarks (Singapore, Barcelona, Seoul, Dubai, etc.)
- Identify content gaps: frameworks, KPIs, case studies, policy templates
- Find latest smart city trends, standards, awards criteria
- Research what makes a smart city resource "award-worthy"

## Stage 4 — Report Writing (report-writing skill)
- Compile findings into a comprehensive critical report
- Prioritized action items (critical → nice-to-have)
- Content bible: recommended additions with research-backed suggestions
- Award submission strategy

## Deliverables
- Full audit report with screenshots
- Prioritized fix list
- Content bible with researched additions
- Award readiness assessment
