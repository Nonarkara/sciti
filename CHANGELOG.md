# Changelog

## 2.0.0

The site is a chooser. It asks which cities, and it answers with published figures.

- Seven areas remain the baseline: environment, economy, mobility, energy, people, living, governance.
- Lenses weight those figures in the open. A missing figure stays missing. A yes or no does not, by itself, rank the map.
- Old award pages, the bingo page, and invented city scores are gone. Old addresses redirect.
- A file in `data/incoming` with a source, a publisher, a date and a licence fills the baseline on the next ingest.
- The cited file adds investment, border trade, students, doctors, visitors, receipts, electricity, output for 2021–2023, and the Treasury appraisal. Lenses rank on those figures. A missing figure is still not a zero.
- The appraisal is compared with the lens mark and labelled only as an official valuation. It is not a market price.
- Quiet retirement and families mark Yala, Pattani and Narathiwat where the emergency decree still covers part of the province, and move them down. That line is the Cabinet map, not a crime count. Provincial crime stays empty. Songkhla is not scored as a security zone. The same line is on remote workers and tourism, and the ranking row carries the mark.
- A province missing an input is shown as, for example, 5 of 7 inputs. It is ranked after every province with the full set, and below 80 percent it is not ranked. A blank is not a zero. An n.a. cell is missing, including Si Sa Ket's unregistered share.
- New companies are cited from data.go.th dataset_12_0111. The 2568 monthly totals match the figures in the file.
- The public pages are in English, Thai and Chinese.
